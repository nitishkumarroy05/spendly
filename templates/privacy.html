Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: Email address and password (hashed for security).
  • Expense data: All expense entries you log, including date, amount, category, and optional descriptions.
  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with the app, such as pages visited and features used.

How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Spendly service and enable you to track your expenses.
  • To display your spending history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • To improve the service through anonymous usage analytics.
  • To send you service-related notifications (e.g., password reset, account recovery).

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal financial data to any third party. We will never use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on encrypted servers. We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, your expense records will be permanently removed from our servers.

Third Party Services

Spendly uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: For typography. No personal data is shared.
  • Web hosting: Your data is hosted on secure cloud infrastructure.

These third parties are bound by confidentiality agreements and are not permitted to use your information for any purpose other than providing the services we request.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about your privacy or this policy, please reach out to us at:

  • Email: support@spendly.local

We will respond to your inquiry within 7 business days.
